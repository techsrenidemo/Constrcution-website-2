What We Build

Our Services
& Capabilities

End-to-end industrial construction and infrastructure solutions across Maharashtra — delivered by specialists, backed by experience.

🏗️
01

RCC Work & Civil Construction

Comprehensive reinforced concrete construction for industrial facilities — from foundations to full superstructures. We use high-grade cement, aggregates, and steel to deliver durable, load-bearing structures that meet engineering specifications. Our teams handle column casting, slab work, retaining walls, and complete building shells.

Foundations RCC Columns Slabs & Beams Retaining Walls
🧱
02

Brick Masonry & Plastering

Damp-proof mortar plaster applied with precision over all masonry surfaces, creating firm, smooth finishes that enhance appearance and structural longevity. We deploy specialist plasterers to eliminate the risk of costly mistakes — ensuring surfaces are level, bonded correctly, and ready for final finishes.

Brick Masonry External Plaster Internal Plaster Damp Proofing
⚙️
03

Heavy Machine Foundations

Precision-engineered concrete foundations for heavy industrial machinery — presses, CNC equipment, turbines, generators, and compressors. Our foundation designs account for dynamic loads, vibration isolation, and anchor bolt placement, ensuring machinery operates at peak performance without settlement or misalignment.

Machine Beds Anchor Bolts Vibration Isolation Grouting
🏭
04

Industrial Sheds & PEB

Pre-Engineered Buildings for manufacturing plants, fabrication facilities, distribution warehouses, showrooms, and data centers. We use high-quality structural steel and certified cladding materials. Our expert teams handle design-to-installation with complete guidance, delivering structurally sound industrial environments with positive track records.

PEB Structures Manufacturing Plants Warehouses Data Centers
🚜
05

Machine Shifting Services

Expert industrial machinery relocation from one bay or facility to another — safely, within stipulated timelines. Our specialized rigging teams use cranes, fork-lift gantries, and precision jacking systems. We transfer machines without harm to any component, using innovative approaches tailored to each machine's specifications and weight.

Plant Relocation Crane Rigging Heavy Lifting Bay Transfer
🛣️
06

Roads & Maintenance Works

Construction and maintenance of all road types within MIDC zones and industrial estates — including approach roads, internal roads, and drainage. Also providing comprehensive industrial maintenance covering inspections, repairs, workspace management, and facility upkeep ensuring operations run without interruption.

MIDC Roads Approach Roads Facility Maintenance Drainage Works
⚡
07

Equipment & DG Rentals

Flexible rental solutions for JCB excavators, tractors, dumpers, and all types of diesel generating sets. Available for short-term and long-term project requirements across Pune's industrial belt. Our maintained equipment ensures reliability at your site without downtime — supporting construction, maintenance, and emergency power needs.

JCB Dumpers DG Sets Tractors
🏘️
08

MIDC Plot Sales & Rentals

Facilitating industrial plot sales and shed rentals across MIDC zones in Pune district — including Warale, Nighoje, Wasuli, and surrounding areas. We connect industry seekers with appropriate industrial real estate including raw house rentals and ready-to-occupy sheds, supporting new business establishment in Maharashtra's industrial zones.

MIDC Plots Shed Rentals Raw House Industrial Real Estate
